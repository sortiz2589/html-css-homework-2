About Miles

Miles Morales is a young superhero who protects his community as Spider-Man. He learns to balance everyday life with responsibility, and he brings his own style, creativity, and courage to being a hero.

Miles uses his abilities to help the people around him.

Characteristics

  1. Brave and determined
  2. Creative and curious
  3. Protective of his family and friends
  4. Learning to trust himself
  5. Committed to helping his neighborhood

Friends and Allies

  • Peter Parker — a mentor and fellow Spider-Man
  • Ganke Lee — his close friend
  • Rio Morales — his mother
  • Jefferson Davis — his father
